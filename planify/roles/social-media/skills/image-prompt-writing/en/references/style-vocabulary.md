# Style vocabulary

Pick what fits; do not use everything at once.

## Medium
photograph, film photo, flat illustration, vector illustration, isometric illustration, 3D render, clay style, watercolour, oil painting, pencil sketch, line art, paper cut, pixel art, low poly

## Shot size and angle
extreme close-up, close-up, medium close-up, half body, full body, wide shot, long shot; eye level, high angle, low angle, bird's-eye view, over the shoulder, first person

## Light
soft natural light, window side light, backlit silhouette, golden hour, blue hour, overcast diffuse light, studio softbox, hard light with strong shadows, neon light, candlelight

## Mood
calm, warm, fresh, bright, mysterious, retro, futuristic, minimal, lively, comforting

## Colour
muted, saturated, pastel, black and white, monochrome, complementary colours, cool tones, warm tones, brand colour + neutrals

## Composition
centred and symmetrical, rule of thirds, negative space, leading lines, frame within a frame, foreground framing, shallow depth of field with blurred background
